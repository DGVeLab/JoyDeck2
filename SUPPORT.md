# Support

For setup, read the [documentation](Docs/README.md) and [troubleshooting guide](Docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

Report reproducible problems through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/DGVeLab/JoyDeck2/issues). Include Hub and firmware versions and steps to reproduce the issue. Feature ideas are welcome through the feature-request form.

For activation enquiries or private support, contact **dgvelab@gmail.com**. Do not post activation codes, tokens, verification codes or personal account details in public issues.

JoyDeck is a free independent maker project. Support is provided as time permits.
